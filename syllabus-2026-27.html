Class 9 Syllabus & Textbook Guide

A reference hub for the new Grade 9 curriculum. Use your school’s subject combination and latest teacher instructions as the final checklist.

Important: NCERT describes its Grade 9 syllabus PDFs as draft documents for school academic planning. Schools may set their own subject combinations, sequence and assessment plan. Confirm the exact syllabus with your school before exam preparation.

Core academic subjects

Mathematics

Ganita Manjari · Parts I & II

Build number sense, algebraic thinking, geometry, measurement, data handling and mathematical reasoning. Practise explaining each step, not only writing the final answer.

NCERT textbook portal
Science

Exploration

Integrated science learning connects physics, chemistry, biology and Earth/environmental science through observation, experiments, evidence and real-life applications.

NCERT textbook portal
Social Science

Understanding Society: India and Beyond

Study society through historical, geographical, economic, civic and social perspectives. Use the latest official NCERT release only; check the exact part prescribed by your school.

AI and skill education

Course code 417

Artificial Intelligence (optional skill subject)

CBSE’s 2026–27 scheme lists AI (417) among optional skill subjects. It is not automatically the same course for every Grade 9 student. Check whether your school has selected it.

  • Employability skills: communication, self-management, ICT, entrepreneurship and green skills.
  • Subject-specific areas in the current curriculum include AI reflection, project cycle and ethics, data literacy, mathematics for AI, generative AI and introductory Python.

How to use this guide

  1. Confirm your school’s subject list and prescribed textbook editions.
  2. Download the relevant official syllabus and mark the topics your teacher has included.
  3. Study the textbook, then use our original notes and practice material for revision.
  4. Ask your teacher about any school-specific deletions, additions or exam pattern.
